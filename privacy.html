CINEMASH — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

CINEMASH is a movie-guessing game. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

What we collect

  • Account information (optional): if you create an account, we store your email address, chosen player name, and avatar selection. You can play without an account.
  • Gameplay statistics: levels cleared, wins/losses, best streak, fastest solve — used for your profile and the global rankings.
  • Anonymous usage data: session counts and minutes played per day, linked to your account ID only, used to understand overall game activity.

What we do NOT collect

  • No location, contacts, photos, or device files.
  • No advertising identifiers — CINEMASH shows no ads.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with third parties.

Where data lives

Account and gameplay data are stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), protected by security rules so only you can write your own data. The movie catalog is public, static data.

Your choices

  • Play without an account — everything then stays on your device.
  • Delete your data: request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at the email below; deletion completes within 30 days.

Children

CINEMASH is a general-audience trivia game with no chat, no ads, and no purchases.
